Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

crusades.io is a free browser strategy game, run from Australia. This page says what information the game collects when you use it, why, who else is involved, and what you can ask us to do about it. If anything here is unclear, write to privacy@crusades.io.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We then know the house name you typed and, like any website, your IP address.
  • If you make an account, we keep your email address and what you own in the game.
  • We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • The site shows advertising from Google, which uses cookies. You can turn off personalised ads.
  • We do not sell your information.

What we collect

When you play

  • Your house name. The name you type before playing. Other players in your game see it.
  • Your IP address and connection times. The game server records these in its log when you connect and disconnect.
  • What you do in a game. Your orders (where you attack, what you build) are sent to the server and passed to the other players in that game so that everyone's game stays in step. They are kept while the game runs so that a player who loses their connection can rejoin.
  • Fault reports. If the game fails to start, loses its connection or stops responding, your browser tells the server what happened (for example "page stalled for 5 seconds"), with your house name, so that we can find and fix faults.

If you make an account

Accounts are optional and are only offered while the store is open.

  • Your email address. Used to send you a sign-in link. There is no password.
  • Your reserved house name, the coat of arms you designed, the items you own and which you have chosen to wear. Other players see your name, your arms and what you wear.
  • Your balance and purchase history: what you bought, when, and for how much.

If you buy something

Payments are taken by Stripe, which may act as the seller of record. Your card or wallet details go to Stripe and never reach us. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded or was refunded, the amount, and which pack it was for, so that we can credit your account. Stripe's own policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

What your browser stores

  • Your seat in a game, so that a refresh or a dropped connection puts you back in the same game. This is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Your sign-in, if you have an account, so that you stay signed in.
  • Advertising cookies set by Google, described below.

Advertising

The landing page, the lobby and the end-of-game screen may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Ads are never shown over the map or while a game is being played.

  • Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com, and from many other ad companies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your choices about advertising cookies when you first visit, and you can change them at any time from the link that message provides.

Who else is involved

We use a small number of companies to run the game. Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

These companies may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Games in progress are held in the server's memory and discarded when the game ends.
  • Server logs (IP addresses, connection times, fault reports) are deleted after 90 days.
  • Account data is kept until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and what goes with it. Write to privacy@crusades.io from the email address on the account and we will act on it within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights, for example under the Australian Privacy Act, the GDPR in Europe and the United Kingdom, or the CCPA in California: to object to or restrict some uses, to take your data elsewhere, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your privacy regulator. In Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). We would like the chance to put things right first, so please write to us.

Children

crusades.io is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to the site and to the game server are encrypted. Account data is held by Supabase with access limited to each account's owner. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top. For a significant change, we will say so on the site's front page.
