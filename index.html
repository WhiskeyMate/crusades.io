How to rule

Click unclaimed land or a neighbour to march on it with the share of your levies set on the slider. Click land across the water to send longships.

Right-click any realm for diplomacy: pacts, gifts, trade bans.

Build with the bar below or keys 1–0, then click the map. Click an existing building of the same kind to enlarge it.

Towns raise your levy cap. Keeps harden your borders. Harbours and markets bring gold. A mage tower lets you hurl fireballs, loose a dragon or call down a starfall; ballista towers shoot them out of the sky.

Camera: drag to pan, wheel to zoom, right-drag or Q/E/R/F to turn and tilt, WASD to move, C to return home. Space pauses. [ and ] change the slider.

Hold four fifths of the realm to win.

Click anywhere to close.

The war is on the map

No battle screens and no menus full of numbers. Everything that happens, happens on the land in front of you.

⚔

Watch the line move

Send a share of your levies at a neighbour and companies of spearmen form up on the border, push it forward tile by tile, and meet the defenders coming the other way.

🏰

Build a realm, not a blob

Towns raise your levy cap, keeps harden a border, harbours and markets fill your purse. Roads appear between your holdings and caravans walk them.

🐉

Sorcery for the endgame

Raise a mage tower and you can hurl fireballs, loose a dragon on a province, or pull down a starfall. Ballista towers are how your rivals answer.

🤝

Pacts, and breaking them

Swear a pact, lend levies, send gold, ban trade. Break your word and every lord in the realm will know you for an oathbreaker.

🗺

A new realm every game

Continents, island chains, twin shores or a ring around an inland sea. Each is generated fresh from a seed you can share.

⛵

The sea matters

Longships carry an invasion to any coast. War galleys sink them, and seize the merchant cogs that make your rivals rich.

How a game goes

  1. Raise your banner

    Pick any unclaimed land. A dozen kingdoms and a hundred clans are doing the same.

  2. Push the line

    Take the wilds, then the clans, then choose which neighbour to trust and which to break.

  3. Take the crown

    Hold four fifths of the realm and it is yours.

What you build

Against people

Today you play against lords run by the game: they expand, ally, betray and cast as a player would, at four levels of ruthlessness.

Multiplayer is being built. Every player's game will run the same simulation in step, so many banners can share one realm without a heavy server.
